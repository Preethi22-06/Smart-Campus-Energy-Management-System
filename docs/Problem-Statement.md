# Smart Campus Energy Management System

## Problem

In colleges and hostels, lights and fans are often left switched ON after classrooms become empty.

Since colleges have multiple buildings and hundreds of rooms, it is difficult for staff members to inspect every room manually before leaving.

This results in:
- Electricity wastage
- Higher electricity bills
- Extra work for maintenance staff
- No centralized monitoring system

## Proposed Solution

Develop a Smart Campus Energy Management System that allows maintenance staff and administrators to monitor classroom electrical appliances through a dashboard.

The dashboard should display the status of lights and fans room-wise, so staff only need to visit rooms where appliances are still ON.
